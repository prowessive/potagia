Import & export

Your data stays
portable.

Move records in and out of your workspace with support for CSV, JSON, and YAML. Keep your information useful beyond a single screen or workflow.

  • Work with familiar formatsCSV for tables, JSON for applications, and YAML for readable structured content.
  • Validate before importingPreview changes and identify row-specific issues before records are saved.
  • Keep access rules intactImports and exports follow the same project permissions as the rest of your workspace.
